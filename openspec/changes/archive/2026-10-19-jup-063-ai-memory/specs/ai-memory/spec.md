## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Bounded AI memory contribution

The AI memory contribution SHALL cover solution flow and model decisions in
sections e/g, refer to the implemented revision and preserve the single editable
source outside Git. Repository artifacts SHALL contain evidence and coverage,
not a second copy of the memory.

#### Scenario: The documentary candidate is prepared

- **WHEN** JUP-063 is prepared for review
- **THEN** its evidence identifies the technical base, paragraphs and external extract
- **AND** other sections and historical comparisons remain unchanged

### Requirement: Three-part brief coverage with provenance

The contribution SHALL map its paragraphs to the official brief's deliverable
description, relevant technical and functional requirements and evaluation
breakdown. Supplied coverage SHALL be distinguished from direct verification.
An unavailable reference SHALL remain an explicit pending check.

#### Scenario: Only supplied coverage is available

- **WHEN** the preparer cannot read the official brief directly
- **THEN** the record attributes the supplied matrix and requests the reference
- **AND** it does not claim an independent contrast or guess requirements

### Requirement: Measurements retain their limits

Evidence SHALL retain original denominators, failures, partial campaigns,
provenance and measurement conditions. Agente judgments SHALL NOT be reported
as human acceptance, nor configuration as proven activation or client outcomes.

#### Scenario: Later dashboard data expands the cohorts

- **WHEN** a supplementary dashboard contains more measurements
- **THEN** the incorporated historical snapshot retains its scope and figures
- **AND** supplementary data does not retroactively accept memory prose

### Requirement: Immutable extract and human closure

The record SHALL identify external dated PDF and Markdown SHA-256 values,
distinguish local reproduction from native export, and separate section pages
from the complete memory's 20-page limit. JUP-063 SHALL remain open while
mandatory human review, validation, contribution or final acceptance is missing.

#### Scenario: Only the e/g extract has been checked

- **WHEN** the external extract has five local pages
- **THEN** the global page limit is not reported as verified
- **AND** human review and validation must name the reviewed date and hash in Doc and Trello

#### Scenario: Automated documentary checks pass

- **WHEN** OpenSpec and repository checks succeed before human closure
- **THEN** no archival, publication or card closure is inferred from those checks
- **AND** archival requires specific human authorization at the CONTRIBUTING stage before PR reviews, without implying human acceptance of the deliverable
