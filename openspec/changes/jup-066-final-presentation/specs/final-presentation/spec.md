## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Timed defense with all team members

The preparation SHALL cover problem, solution, demo, architecture, results and
future work with proposed active speaking parts for all four Economicon members.
It SHALL fit the official 10–20 minute interval by budget and distinguish planned
duration from observed rehearsal time.

#### Scenario: Review the proposed running order

- **WHEN** the team reads the script
- **THEN** it finds six timed blocks totaling 18 minutes and a 15-minute fallback
- **AND** all four members retain speaking parts in both schedules
- **AND** the official question-time interpretation remains pending confirmation.

### Requirement: Demonstration evidence preserves its identity

The script SHALL reuse the pinned JUP-065 demonstration, preserving its public
sample, monthly UI selection, daily reference window and seven-minute budget.
It SHALL distinguish offline expectations, historical recordings and live results.

#### Scenario: No integrated rehearsal is available

- **WHEN** the team has only the prepared package
- **THEN** it uses explicitly labelled offline material
- **AND** it does not claim successful integrated execution, real customer data or savings.

#### Scenario: Live provider or evidence fails

- **WHEN** a required demo step fails or is blocked
- **THEN** the presenter states the incident, records its status and switches to the labelled fallback
- **AND** it does not replace the observed answer with the rubric or retry without a time bound.

### Requirement: Claims retain sources and limitations

The preparation SHALL identify the official brief, shared memory revision,
demo commit and evaluation provenance. It SHALL preserve historical mock results
as historical mock results and keep other memory sections in their canonical source.

#### Scenario: Cite the historical evaluation

- **WHEN** the presenter reports zero accepted answer cases
- **THEN** the script identifies 0 of 20 answer cases within 28 total cases,
  the historical date and mock/template mode
- **AND** it does not infer current generative quality or customer impact.

### Requirement: Participation and acceptance are explicit

The preparation SHALL preserve Trello responsibilities and separate suggested
speaking parts from actual authorship, pairing, review and validation.
Rehearsal and individual confirmations SHALL remain pending until observed.

#### Scenario: Deliver a documentary contribution before the human rehearsal

- **WHEN** the contributor hands the script to the assigned leader
- **THEN** the rehearsal sheet retains not_run results and pending personal confirmations
- **AND** the contribution does not constitute an independent validation or completed card.
